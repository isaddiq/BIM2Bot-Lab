BIM2Bot Lab

From IFC model to robot route, entirely in the browser.

BIM2Bot Lab is a free online IFC viewer and construction informatics lab. Open an IFC model to explore its geometry and semantics, turn it into maps a robot can plan on, and simulate robot fleets moving through the building. Every navigation result can be traced back to the IFC elements that produced it. Files are parsed on your device with web-ifc, so nothing is uploaded.

  • Explore: view IFC2x3, IFC4 and IFC4.3 models in 3D and on 2D floor plans, and inspect classes, properties and the spatial structure.
  • Navigate: build room graphs and occupancy grids from spaces, doors and stairs, and plan multi-level A* routes.
  • Simulate: run robot fleets with delivery missions, lift scheduling, congestion and human–robot encounters.
